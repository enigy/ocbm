Carlink 3.20: fuel discounts, and quick phone changes with OCBM 2.6.0

- Offers the adapter update to OCBM 2.6.0, from OCBM 1.0.1 or later, and installs 2.6.0 on a stock adapter (Settings › Adapter and about). With 2.6.0 a change of phone takes about 5 s from the phone joining the adapter's Wi-Fi to the CarPlay picture, against 40-90 s before, and a switch from the Phones page about 9 s from the tap.
- Settings › Vehicle › Fuel discounts, with Nearby stations on: enter the cents off per gallon you get at a listed brand's stations, or at another station by its name as the Fuel card shows it. The Fuel card then ranks those stations by the price you pay, with the posted price beside it.
- A program covers regular to premium, and diesel and E85 too unless you turn "Diesel and E85 too" off. One store of a brand, typed with its number or street, can be a program of its own. Costco and Sam's Club aren't listed: their posted price is already the member price. Discounts are set while parked, and only the amount is kept, never a membership number.
- Phones: the rank arrows show a press, as the pane controls do. A pane control or arrow keeps its full touch area while pressed, so a press near its edge no longer slips off it.
- The loading screen fades out over about a second as CarPlay starts, instead of cutting away.
- Logs: the display-off wait reads in seconds, and a display flicker during a wake no longer leaves a wrong wake line.
- The bench hooks in this version's commits are in debug builds only, never in this release.
